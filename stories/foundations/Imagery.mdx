# 🌱 Imagery

A reference gallery of imagery for mock-ups, decks, and prototypes. Every tile
links to a CDN URL — **no binary assets ship in this package**.

> 🧪 **Placeholder set.** These are free [Lorem Picsum](https://picsum.photos)
> images, not Mind's brand photography. The curated Mind imagery (with
> licensing) will replace them in a single `_assets.ts` edit.

## 🎨 Tone

- 🌅 **Warm and human.** Favour natural light, real environments, and candid
  moments over stock-photo staging.
- 🤫 **Quiet, not loud.** Imagery supports the message; it should never fight the
  UI or the type.
- 🌍 **Inclusive and grounded.** Reach for authentic, diverse, real-world scenes.

## ✅ Do / 🚫 Don't

- ✅ **Do** keep a consistent colour temperature across a single surface.
- ✅ **Do** leave breathing room — let subjects sit comfortably in the frame.
- ✅ **Do** check contrast when text overlays an image (overlay/scrim as needed).
- 🚫 **Don't** stretch or squash — always preserve the source aspect ratio.
- 🚫 **Don't** stack heavy filters; subtle grading only.
- 🚫 **Don't** ship an image without meaningful `alt` text.

## 📐 Recommended aspect ratios

- **4:3** — general gallery and card imagery (the ratio used below).
- **16:9** — hero banners and wide cover images.
- **1:1** — thumbnails, tiles, and avatars.
- **3:4 / 9:16** — portrait and mobile-first surfaces.
