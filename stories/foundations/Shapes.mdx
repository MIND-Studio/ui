# 🔷 Shapes

Shapes covers **radius** today: a single base radius (`--radius` ← `--mind-radius`) drives a
derived scale, so a brand sets one value — Mind `0.625rem`, Ember `1rem`, Arctic `0rem` — and
the whole scale follows. Switch brand in the toolbar to re-round the scale.

There's room to grow this page into **borders and shadows** as those become tokenized.
